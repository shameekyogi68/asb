What We Do

Our Activities & Events

From celebrating womanhood to honouring Dr. Babasaheb's legacy — our Sangha stands for community, dignity, and collective progress in Byndoor.

Education & Awareness

Promoting education, literacy, and health awareness among women and children of the Byndoor community.

Community Welfare

Distributing essential supplies, supporting residential school students, and fostering solidarity among community members.

Women's Empowerment

Honouring local women achievers, conducting health and self-care programs, and building self-confidence among women.

Commemorations

Observing Mahaparinirvana Diwas, Ambedkar Jayanti, and other significant dates to keep the Ambedkarite spirit alive.

Health Programs

Organising medical camps, health talks, and wellness sessions especially focused on women's health needs.

Social Solidarity

Building bridges across the community, bringing together volunteers, guests, and dignitaries for a shared vision of equality.

About the Sangha

Who We Are

A registered women's organisation in Byndoor, Karnataka, working to uphold the values of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — equality, fraternity, and the dignity of every woman.

01

Registered Organisation

We are a formally registered Mahila Sangha (Reg. No. 2830647) operating in Byndoor, Udupi District, Karnataka.

02

Ambedkarite Values

Founded on the principles of "Educate, Organise, Agitate" — we champion equality, justice, and self-reliance for all women.

03

Community Welfare Focus

From distributing essentials to residential school students to hosting health awareness sessions — we act where it matters most.

04

Women-Led Leadership

Led by President Mrs. Sushila and Secretary Mrs. Renuka, our Sangha is fully managed by committed women from the community.

05

Inclusive & Open

Membership is open to all women of Byndoor who share our belief in social equality and Ambedkarite ideals.

06

Grassroots Impact

Every program we run is local, personal, and community-driven — no bureaucracy, just real people making real change.
